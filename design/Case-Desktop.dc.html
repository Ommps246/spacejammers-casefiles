Other suspects

What else could explain it?

Suspect 1 · checkedCorrelation, not causation

Rainfall variability

Do drier months explain warm months? We line up rain and temperature month by month. A match would show they move together, not that one causes the other.

Devil’s AdvocateObjection

This data averages a ~50 km area and blends satellite and model inputs, so it can hide city-scale heat. The 1997 shift coincides with the 1997–98 El Niño and a satellite instrument change.

We keep this objection on the record. It is why the verdict stays Inconclusive.

Verdict · Case 01
Inconclusive

Chennai shows slight warming, but not enough to call it yet

Inconclusive is a finding, not a failure. The evidence is on file; it just doesn’t clear the bar to rule out chance. We’d rather say so than overstate it.

What we can say

Chennai’s average is edging up, +0.065 °C per decade. Over the same years the whole planet warmed at +0.211 °C per decade.

What we can’t say yet

That Chennai’s trend is more than chance, or that 1997 was a real turning point (p = 0.17).

What would change it

Sharper, city-scale evidence: MODIS satellite land temperature at 1 km.
